1. Who we are

Visto Apps is the brand of Darius Cirjan, the developer and data controller. This policy explains what the Darc app and its Safari extension (iPhone, iPad and Mac) and the website https://darcapp.com do with personal data.

Contact for any privacy question or request: iosallapps@gmail.com. Website: vistoapps.com. We answer within one month (art. 12 GDPR). We are not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer.

Last updated: November 15, 2026.

2. At a glance

  • No account. Darc does not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • The Safari extension recolours pages on your device. Your browsing history and page content are never sent to us, and there is no Darc server to send them to. The extension sends nothing to any third party.
  • The Darc app on iPhone and iPad shows ads from Google AdMob to people who have not unlocked it. Personalised ads only with your consent; in the EEA, UK and Switzerland no ad loads before you answer the consent form. The Mac app shows no ads.
  • The app measures how it is used (Google Analytics for Firebase) and sends crash reports. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland usage statistics start only after the consent form has been answered and allowed.
  • If iCloud is on, your settings and site rules, including the host names of sites you set a rule for, sync through your own iCloud account.
  • We do not sell personal data for money. We have no servers of our own that receive your data.

3. Data that stays on your device

Safari extension. To recolour a page, the extension needs Safari's permission to access websites (it is declared for all websites, with the storage, scripting, tabs, activeTab and native messaging permissions). It runs inside every page you open in Safari, including embedded frames, from the moment the page starts loading, and reads the page's colours and styles and the page's address in order to decide how to theme it. It may load the page's own stylesheets again from the website that serves them; those requests go to the website you are visiting, never to us. It does not send form entries or passwords, and it sends no page content, address or browsing history to us or to any other party. The extension has no network code of its own and talks only to the Darc app on your device. When you press Report issue, it opens a pre-filled email for you to read and send yourself, containing the site's host name (not the full address), the mode and engine Darc used, whether the site was already dark, the Darc version and the platform.

Stored on your device. Darc keeps in the Safari extension's storage and in an app group shared with the Darc app: your global mode, dark hours, fine-tuning values, and your site rules (the host name of each site you set a rule for, its mode and its fine-tuning), plus small status data such as when a page was last themed and whether onboarding was completed. To open each site in the right theme without a white flash, the extension also remembers, for each website it themed, the host name (not the full address or anything from the page), the last theme used there and whether theming failed there; this list is kept in the extension's own storage, is deleted automatically for a site not themed for 30 days, is never sent anywhere and is not synced through iCloud. The app also keeps whether Darc is unlocked or was bought as a paid app, so it works offline, and your answers in Google's consent form. If iCloud is on, settings and site rules also sync through your iCloud account (see the table). This data can be part of your iCloud or computer backup, which Apple's own policy covers.

How to remove it: in Darc, open a site and choose Reset site to remove its rule; deleting the app removes the data on the device. Turning iCloud off for Darc stops syncing; a copy that is already in your iCloud account stays there under Apple's policy.

5. Permissions

The app asks for these permissions only when you use the feature, and works without them. Change them anytime in your device settings.

  • Website access (Safari extension). Needed for Darc to work: Safari asks you to let the extension access websites, and warns that it can read and alter web pages. Darc asks for all websites so it can recolour any page you open; you can limit it to chosen sites in iOS Settings, Apps, Safari, Extensions, Darc, or in Safari on the Mac under Settings, Extensions. See "Data that stays on your device" above for exactly what the extension reads and where that goes.
  • Tracking (iOS, App Tracking Transparency). Asked once, after the consent form. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised with it.

6. Consent, ads and tracking

On iPhone and iPad, in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app first shows Google's consent form (a certified consent management platform under the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework). Until you answer it, no ad is requested and no usage statistics are sent. If you refuse personalised ads, you still get limited, non-personalised ads.

On iPhone and iPad the App Tracking Transparency prompt comes after the consent form. Ads load only once it is answered.

Change your choice anytime: in the app, Help, Privacy, Ad Privacy Settings (shown only where the consent form applies). Also Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Analytics in those regions also need your consent to storing information on the device and to measuring performance; if you do not give it, analytics stays off. Where no consent regime applies, usage statistics run without a form.

The Mac app shows no ads and has no consent form; it measures usage only when the App Store country is outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. Unlocking Darc removes the ads and the consent form on iPhone and iPad.

7. Purchases

Purchases are made through App Store. The store processes the payment; we receive only a pseudonymous receipt, checked through RevenueCat, that tells the app which purchase you own. We never see your card or account details.

8. How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you remove a site rule with Reset site or delete the app. Deleting the app does not by itself remove a copy already kept in your iCloud account. Data held by the services above is kept for the period listed in the table. We do not keep copies of our own. Emails you send us, including Report issue emails, are kept as long as needed to answer, and at most 2 years.

9. Transfers outside the EEA

Some providers (Google, RevenueCat and others marked in the table) process data in the United States or other countries. Transfers rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for certified companies or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (art. 45 and 46 GDPR).

10. Your rights (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing (including to object at any time to processing based on legitimate interest), to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing.

Because the app has no account, we usually cannot tell which data at Google or RevenueCat is yours. Write to iosallapps@gmail.com and, if you can, include an Apple order number for a purchase, and we will forward the request to the provider. You can remove the data on the device by deleting the app.

You can complain to the supervisory authority where you live or work, or to ours: Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal (ANSPDCP), www.dataprotection.ro. The list of EU authorities: edpb.europa.eu.

11. United States

  • We do not sell personal information for money.
  • When you allow personalised ads, Google AdMob uses your advertising identifier for ads based on activity in other apps. Some US state laws (for example California) call this "sharing" or "targeted advertising". You can opt out at any time: refuse tracking on iOS (Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking), or email us and we will treat it as an opt-out request.
  • Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws can ask to know, correct or delete their personal information, and will not be treated differently for doing so. Categories collected in the last 12 months are the ones in the table above (identifiers, internet activity, approximate location from IP, purchase records). Requests: iosallapps@gmail.com.

12. Children

Darc is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, or under the age of digital consent in their country (13 to 16 in the EU). If you believe a child gave us data, write to iosallapps@gmail.com and we will delete what we can reach. Ads in the app are not designed for children.

13. Security

All network connections use HTTPS. Data on the device is protected by the operating system's app sandbox and encryption. No system is perfectly secure, so we keep what leaves your device to the minimum listed here.

14. This website

darcapp.com is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA), which records the IP address of visitors in server logs for security. The site sets no cookies, uses no analytics or ads, loads no third-party scripts or fonts, and stores nothing in your browser. Its links to the App Store lead to Apple's own pages.

15. Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, we update this page and the date at the top before the new version ships. Important changes are also mentioned in the store update notes.
