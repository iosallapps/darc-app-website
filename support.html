Turn on Darc on iPhone and iPad

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Go to Apps > Safari > Extensions > Darc.
  3. Turn on Allow Extension.
  4. Set Other Websites to Allow, so Darc can theme every site.
  5. Open any website in Safari. The Darc app shows that the extension is running once it has themed a page.

You can also reach the same place from Safari: tap the page menu button in the address bar, then Manage Extensions. In the Darc app, Open Safari Extensions takes you straight to Safari’s extension settings on iOS 26.2 and later. On earlier versions the button reads Open Settings and opens Darc’s own page in Settings, from where you go to Apps, Safari, Extensions.

Turn on Darc on Mac

  1. Open the Darc app once.
  2. In Safari, choose Safari > Settings, then click Extensions.
  3. Tick the checkbox next to Darc.
  4. Click Edit Websites and set Other Websites to Allow.

A site looks wrong

Open the site in Safari and tap or click Darc’s button in the toolbar. The menu starts with the site’s name and its modes. Default follows what you chose for all websites. System, Dark and Light give the site its own rule. Off leaves the site untouched, and you can change it back from the same menu or the Sites tab in the app. Under the modes are two buttons:

  • Reset site removes the rule for that site, including its fine-tuning and media settings, so it follows your setting for all websites again.
  • Report issue opens a pre-filled email in your mail app. It contains the site’s host name (not the full address), the mode and engine Darc used, whether Darc found the site already dark, the Darc version and the platform. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself.

On a subdomain, such as m.example.com, the menu may say it uses the rule for example.com. The modes then change that rule. If the menu says your rule for a site is paused, the free version is not applying it: it keeps your 3 oldest site rules active and pauses the rest. Reset site removes a paused rule, and Darc Forever turns it back on.

If theming goes wrong on a page, for example text that becomes hard to read, Darc removes what it added and the page goes back to how the site built it.

A short white flash when a page starts loading comes from Safari, which paints its own placeholder before the page exists. No extension can restyle that placeholder.

Darc is on but nothing changes

Set Other Websites to Allow, then open any site. The app shows Darc is on after its first page.

Also check the mode. In System, Darc follows your device’s appearance, so a bright site stays bright while the device is in light mode. A site that already has its own dark theme is left to use it.

Why Safari asks for website access

When you allow Darc, Safari warns that the extension can read and alter web pages. Safari shows that warning for every extension that changes how pages look. To restyle a page, Darc has to read its colours and styles. It does that inside Safari on your device. Darc has no servers, and it never sends page content or browsing history anywhere. The privacy policy has the details.

Restore your purchase

Darc Forever is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple Account, and it covers iPhone, iPad and Mac. On a new device, or after reinstalling, open the Darc app, go to the Help tab and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund, use reportaproblem.apple.com.
