About that Safari warning

Safari asks. Here is the honest answer.

When you turn Darc on, Safari warns that the extension can read and alter web pages, including passwords and other sensitive details. Safari shows that message for every extension that changes how pages look, because it is technically true: to restyle a page, Darc has to read its colours.

What Darc does with that access is small and stays on your device.

What Darc does

Reads a page's styles. Changes its colours. That is all.

The work happens inside Safari on your iPhone, iPad or Mac.

  • No servers. Darc has nowhere to send your data, and it sends none.
  • Your browsing stays yours. Page content and history never leave Safari.
  • No account, analytics, ads or tracking. Not in the app, not in the extension.
  • Your settings, your iCloud. If iCloud is on, settings and site rules sync through your own account.

The full details are in the privacy policy.

Questions

Good to know

Why does Darc need access to all websites?

To restyle a page, Darc has to read its colours, so Safari asks for access on each site where it runs. Everything happens on your device. Page content and browsing history never leave Safari, and Darc has no servers to send them to.

How is this different from Safari's own dark mode?

Safari follows each website's own design. If a site has no dark version, it stays bright. Darc builds a dark theme for sites that do not have one, and with Darc Forever it builds a light theme for sites that only come in dark.

What does System mode do?

It follows your device appearance. When your iPhone, iPad or Mac switches to Dark Mode, bright sites turn dark. In light appearance, sites keep their own look. With Darc Forever, dark-only sites are lightened as well, and you can set dark hours instead.

A site looks wrong. What can I do?

Tap Darc's button in Safari's toolbar. Reset site removes that site's rule so it follows your global settings again, Disable for this site leaves it untouched from then on, and Report issue opens an email to us with the site's name so we can look at it. More on the support page.

Why do I see a short white flash sometimes?

Safari paints its own white placeholder before a page exists, and no extension can restyle it. The page itself is themed from its first frame.

Is it a subscription?

No. Darc is free to use, and Darc Forever is a one-time purchase. There are no ads either way.

Do I need to buy it again on my Mac or iPad?

No. One purchase unlocks Darc Forever on iPhone, iPad and Mac signed in with the same Apple Account. Use Restore Purchases in the app's Help tab if it does not show up right away.
