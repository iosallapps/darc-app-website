2. What Darc does

Darc is a Safari extension, with a companion app, for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It gives bright websites a dark theme and dark-only websites a light theme, can follow your device's appearance or a schedule, lets you set a mode per site, and adjusts brightness, contrast, saturation and sepia. It works only inside Safari. The Safari extension needs your permission to access websites, and it recolours pages on your device.

6. Important: results and your responsibility

Darc recolours pages automatically and can get a page wrong, for example text that stays hard to read, a photo that looks off, or a brief white flash before the page darkens. If it gets a page wrong you can turn Darc off for that site or reset it. It is meant for comfort, not for medical or accessibility needs.
